    Glossary

    Vesting Date
    The legal date the new unitary authority comes into existence. All Tier 1 (Day 1 critical) services must be operational by this date. Contract notice deadlines are measured relative to it.
    Predecessor
    An existing council being merged or restructured. Its IT estate, contracts, and services are inherited by one or more successor authorities.
    Successor
    The new unitary authority created from the reorganisation. A predecessor can be full (all systems transfer) or partial (systems split across multiple successors).
    Perspective
    A filtered view of the estate. "All" shows the full merged picture. Selecting a specific successor or council filters analysis to that entity.
    Collision
    Two or more councils have IT systems serving the same function. Collisions require rationalisation decisions.
    Cross-tier
    Systems from councils with different tier classifications (county vs district). May represent complementary delivery, not duplication.
    Portability

    High Open APIs, native interop — easy extraction

    Medium Batch/CSV/SQL exports — moderate effort

    Low Proprietary lock-in — high vendor hostage risk

    Data Layer

    Segmented Partitions cleanly by ward/department

    Monolithic Entangled across domains — requires ETL

    Cloud / On-Premise

    Cloud Externally hosted SaaS/IaaS — portable

    On-prem Council servers — migration, licensing, support risk

    ERP
    Enterprise Resource Planning — monolithic business system (finance, HR, payroll). High data entanglement risk. TCoP Point 9 concern.
    Shared Service
    A system jointly operated across council boundaries. Requires unwinding or re-contracting. May cross successor boundaries.
    Capability Platform
    A system that provides shared capabilities (payments, notifications, forms) consumed by other systems. Decommissioning creates blast radius across dependents.
    ESD/LGA Function
    Standard function from the LGA/ESD taxonomy (176 entries). Maps council-specific names to a common classification for cross-council comparison.
    Contract Urgency
    Classifies contract timing relative to vesting: Pre-vesting Year 1 Natural expiry Long-tail
    Notice Period Action Zone
    The true decision deadline = expiry minus notice period. A 12-month notice means the decision is due 12 months before contract end.
    User Volume
    Relative system scale by user count. Anchor (50%+ larger) Standard
    Vendor Density
    Same vendor across multiple predecessors for a function. Commercial opportunity to consolidate into a single agreement.
    Tech Debt (On-premise)
    Systems hosted on council servers. On-prem Cloud
    Data Monolith
    Systems with monolithic data partitioning or ERP status. Monolithic / ERP Segmented
    Data Portability
    Worst portability rating present: Low Medium High
    TCoP Alignment
    Assessment against UK Government Technology Code of Practice. Points 3, 4, 5, 9, and 11 evaluated from system properties.
    Shared Service
    Systems with sharedWith declarations. In transition mode, detects cross-successor boundary sharing.
    Inherit as-is
    Single system, one successor. No rationalisation choice needed — but check signals for modernisation concerns (portability, TCoP, cost).
    Choose & consolidate
    Multiple systems compete for the same function within a successor. Signals highlight differentiators to inform which is retained.
    Extract & partition
    System spans successor boundaries — data must be separated. Monolithic data layers carry the highest extraction risk.
    Extract, partition & consolidate
    Most complex: split across successors AND consolidate within each. Sequence matters — partition first, then consolidate.
    TCoP (Technology Code of Practice)
    UK Government framework. Assessed points: 3 (spend/lock-in), 4 (open standards), 5 (cloud first), 9 (modularity), 11 (commercial flexibility).
    Tier 1 — Day 1 Critical
    Statutory and safeguarding services that must be operational from vesting day.
    Tier 2 — High Priority
    Important post-transition services. Not Day 1 critical but needed early.
    Tier 3 — Post-Day 1
    Can be transitioned after establishment. Promoted to Tier 2 when contract notice trigger falls before vesting.
    Anchor System
    System 50%+ larger than next-largest by user count. Generally the strongest migration consolidation target.

    Playbook Tier Mapping

    Default ESD function-to-tier classification based on MHCLG Playbook prioritisation guidance. Functions not listed default to Tier 2. Function-level overrides and Tier 3 promotion (when a contract expires before vesting) may adjust the effective tier at runtime.

    Custom Workstreams

    Group functions into named workstreams for programme-level tracking. Workstreams appear as cards alongside the ESD taxonomy domains.
