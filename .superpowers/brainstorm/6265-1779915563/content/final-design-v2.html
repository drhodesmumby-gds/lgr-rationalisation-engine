Key change from v1: The grid is ONLY for sharing configuration (checkboxes showing who participates in what). System selection stays in the familiar successor cards — one card per successor with a dropdown, identical to State 1. The grid provides the bird's-eye overview without trying to do double duty as both a system selector AND a sharing configurator. You click a function in Pane 1 → its successor cards appear in Pane 2 → the grid below always shows the cross-function sharing picture.
