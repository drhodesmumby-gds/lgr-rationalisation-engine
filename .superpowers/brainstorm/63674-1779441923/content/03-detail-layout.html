Focus Mode Detail: Tabs vs Grouped Sections

Same data, two ways to present it in the detail panel. Which scans better?

A: Tabbed Groups

Fields split across tabs. Only one group visible at a time. Cleaner but requires clicking to see everything.

Cons: Can't see contract AND technical at same time. Extra clicks to review full system. "Out of sight, out of mind" for less-used fields.

B: Labelled Sections (all visible)

All fields on one scrollable page, separated by section headings. Everything visible without clicking.
