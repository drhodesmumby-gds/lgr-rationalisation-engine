Design principle: Pane 1 shows WHAT (system + which function is active). Pane 2 shows the DECISION (system choice + operating model for that function). Pane 3 shows WHO (successor allocation, cost split, impact) — and adapts based on the operating model selected in Pane 2. Clicking between functions in Pane 1 updates Panes 2 and 3.
